Camino de Santiago in 34 Walking Days｜1,000 km French Way Journal by Brook from Taiwan

In 2026 I walked the Camino Francés: 34 days on foot, about 1,000 km from Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port (SJPP) to Santiago de Compostela, then on to Fisterra and Muxía on the Atlantic coast. Door to door (Taiwan → Paris → SJPP … Madrid → Taiwan) the trip was 40 days: 18 May – 26 June 2026.

This is not a travel-agency brochure. It is a day-by-day record — blisters, heatwaves, red wine, and the kindness of strangers — plus the practical numbers future pilgrims actually need: route, stages, budget, gear and transport.

📊 The trip in numbers

  • Walking: 34 days · ~1,000 km · SJPP → Santiago (~800 km, 29 days) → Fisterra → Muxía
  • Whole journey: 40 days, 18 May – 26 June 2026 (includes flights Taiwan–Paris, trains to SJPP, and Madrid on the way home)
  • Total cost (2026, real ledger): NT$103,227 — pre-trip NT$31,326 (flights, insurance, SIM) + €1,930 on the road + Dubai layover shopping
  • Daily pace on the road: about €35–45 per day (public albergues €10–15, pilgrim menu €10–18)
  • Pack: around 7 kg base weight; luggage transfer available from SJPP (~€8 per stage)

🗺️ Route at a glance

  • Days 1–29: SJPP → Santiago de Compostela, ~800 km over the Pyrenees, Meseta, León mountains and Galicia (crossing Pamplona, Burgos, León, O Cebreiro, Sarria).
  • Days 30–34: Santiago → Negreira → Santa Mariña → Cee → Fisterra (0.00 km, the "end of the world") → Muxía (rebirth).
  • Days 35–37: bus back to Santiago, high-speed train to Madrid, flight home to Taiwan.

❓ Quick answers

How many days does the French Way take?

SJPP → Santiago is ~800 km; most pilgrims allow 30–35 days. I walked 34 days for ~1,000 km including Fisterra and Muxía (40 days door to door).

How much does it cost?

My 2026 total was NT$103,227. On the road, €35–45 per day is comfortable. Full breakdown: budget page (Chinese).

When is the best month?

May–June and September–October are the most stable. I walked May–June and still hit 42°C afternoons on the Meseta — start before sunrise in summer.

Do I need to book albergues?

Public albergues (€10–15) are mostly first-come, first-served. In peak season, check ahead for popular towns.
