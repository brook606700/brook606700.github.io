Camino Francés Guide: Route, Stages, Budget & Preparation (from a 2026 Walk)

First time planning the Camino? I (Brook, Taiwan) walked the Camino Francés in 2026 — 34 days on foot, ~1,000 km from SJPP to Santiago, Fisterra and Muxía. This page condenses what I learned into route, stages, budget, gear, transport and safety. My day-by-day journals are in Traditional Chinese (Day 0 – Day 37); this guide gives you everything in English.

Route: what is the Camino Francés?

The Camino is a network of pilgrim routes ending at Santiago de Compostela. The French Way starts in Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port, crosses the Pyrenees, then passes Pamplona, Logroño, Burgos, León, O Cebreiro and Sarria before Santiago (~800 km). I continued to Fisterra (0.00 km, "the end of the world") and Muxía (~90 km more).

Itinerary: how many days?

  • SJPP → Santiago: ~800 km, commonly 30–35 days depending on daily distance and rest days.
  • + Fisterra & Muxía: ~90 km more, 3–4 days. My total on foot: 34 days, ~1,000 km (40 days door to door).
  • Hardest start: SJPP → Roncesvalles climbs ~1,480 m. Train for it; don't make day one your first long hike.

Budget: how much money?

My real 2026 ledger: NT$103,227 total = pre-trip NT$31,326 (flights Taipei–Paris, insurance, SIM) + €1,930 on the road + Dubai layover. Daily rule of thumb: €35–45 per day — public albergue €10–15, pilgrim menu €10–18, supermarket resupply much cheaper than restaurants. Prices, exchange rates and your route choices will move the total; treat mine as a breakdown template, not a quote.

Gear: what to pack?

  • Keep it light: ~7 kg base weight worked for me (including camera gear). Every kilo multiplies over 30+ days.
  • Shoes + socks first: broken-in shoes and proven socks beat any online ranking; carry blister care from day one.
  • Luggage transfer: available from SJPP (~€8 per stage) — I used it on day one and never regretted it.
  • Full list (Chinese): packing & transfer guide.

Getting to SJPP from Taiwan

My route: Taipei → Paris CDG (Etihad via Abu Dhabi), TGV Paris Montparnasse → Bayonne, TER to SJPP. Allow 4+ hours at CDG immigration, and note the fixed €65 taxi fare into Paris. Details (Chinese): transport guide.

Safety & fitness

Terrain, sun, water stops and bed races vary daily. Check weather, route notices and albergue seasons before you go; carry enough water; cut stages short when your body complains. The numbers in my journals are my records, not prescriptions — plan conservatively.

FAQ

Is the French Way good for first-timers?

Yes — it has the best infrastructure, clearest yellow-arrow waymarking and the most albergues. Still, match daily distance to your fitness and build in rest flexibility.

How much time for SJPP → Santiago?

Commonly 30–35 days for ~800 km. Add 3–4 days if you extend to Fisterra and Muxía like I did (34 walking days total).

What does it cost per day?

Budget €35–45 per day on the road in 2026 terms; flights and season swing the total.
