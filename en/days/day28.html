Day 28｜Walking in the Mist, the Night Before Graduation, Finally a Bottom Bunk

June 17, 2026 · Melide → O Pedrouzo · 33km

Today is day 28: on to O Pedrouzo, 33 kilometers — the last big stop before the finish line in Santiago de Compostela.

I spent the whole day hurrying through thick fog. Since picking up the pace, I hadn't seen a familiar face in ages — then by a waymarker I ran into the Korean friends from before, and they even took this shot of my back! That reunion hit me hard with emotion — maybe because the finish line is right there, and I feel exactly like a student about to graduate tomorrow!

On the road I met that energetic group of students again! Watching their teacher herd the mischievous ones back into line with a stick like a shepherd with sheep was hilarious. As we passed each other they excitedly high-fived me — that youthful fire is truly contagious!

The railings were covered in fabric strips from every country fluttering in the wind — including Taiwan's, of course! And among a bottle wall covered in graffiti, I was thrilled to spot a familiar lucky "good fortune" sticker — every pilgrim who passed here left a story behind. And the best part: after 28 days of walking, I finally got a bottom bunk! No ladder to climb — pure happiness!

📚 Pilgrim's mini-guide: O Pedrouzo & Galician Eucalyptus

O Pedrouzo sits only about 20 kilometers from Santiago — the final night. The most vivid smell-memory of this whole stretch is eucalyptus: Galicia is full of eucalyptus forests, and their fresh scent in the drizzle is the last signature smell before the finish.

Many pilgrims can't sleep on the last night — not from nerves, but from not wanting it to end. Arrive early, rest early, and save your legs for tomorrow's final 20 kilometers.
