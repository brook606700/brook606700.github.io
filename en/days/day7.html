Day 7｜Singing Grandmas, Parasol Mount, and Calle Laurel — Los Arcos → Logroño

May 27, 2026 · Los Arcos → Logroño · 28km

Setting off under a gorgeous dawn. Today, we officially stepped into Spain's famous wine country, Logroño. As my drone rose, I took in this dawn-lit land from a different angle.

These three grandmas on the trail were just adorable. Singing as they walked, occasionally breaking into joyful little dances. That pure happiness and energy was truly the most beautiful scenery of this journey.

Some stretches were overgrown with thorny weeds. Good thing I wore scratch-proof hiking pants—perfect defense against physical attacks.

On the trail I met that hand-in-hand couple again, and today I played third wheel—chatting as we walked, our steps feeling lighter. Arriving in town, we found a Bar for breakfast. Only after stepping inside did I realize the window framed a picture-perfect landscape. Breakfast against that stunning view—this rhythm was pure chill.

After that gorgeous breakfast, back on the road—the heatwave trial continued, facing the high temperatures head-on. I have to rave about my sun-shielding secret weapon: the parasol mount. Hands completely free, walking and sun protection with zero effort. A must-have for hot-weather hiking!

Almost nobody on the Camino carries a parasol. With the mount on, my embarrassment meter exploded—100% head-turn rate. But for perfect defense against the 42-degree heatwave, vanity takes a back seat; those stares were completely worth it.

Arriving in Logroño. Tonight I am staying at the church-run albergue. Its signature feature is an underground tunnel connecting it to the church, and stays are donation-based. Since registration only opens at 2pm, we used the gap to visit the town cathedral first.

While the albergue was still closed, we stocked up at Carrefour. Wine country lives up to its name: an entire wall of red wine to choose from, bottles at just 1 to 5 euros! So many choices it made my head spin.

Finally, check-in began. With limited volunteers, everyone gathered outside the door while the volunteer explained the house rules in one go. No-nonsense, efficient and clear.

Backpacks lined up in formation, everyone registering in turn. Checking my bunk for the night: a bunk bed with no guardrails at all. Sleep well and stay put—the night to test your sleeping posture has arrived.

Albergue tradition night! Everyone joined the church mass together, first receiving the special pilgrim blessing, then gathering for the communal dinner. A rigorous flow with ritual turned all the way up—this is the most authentic Camino experience.

The final ritual before dinner: volunteers teaching everyone the pilgrims' own Ultreia Suseia song, meaning "Onward! Onward! Upward! God help us." Everyone sang together in chorus—only when the whole room had sung it through did dinner officially hit the table. Music bridging languages, atmosphere maxed out.

From mass blessing to chorus dinner, joining this traditional format for the first time maxed out the Camino feeling.

Then came Logroño's main event: the Calle Laurel bar crawl. The standard play here is one bar, one glass, one signature tapas. Finish and move to the next, sweeping the whole street the most authentic way.

You absolutely cannot miss this mushroom tapas—one bite and it bursts juicy in your mouth, flavor exploding everywhere!

The Calle Laurel crawl wrapped up perfectly. A few glasses of red wine in, my steps carried a pleasant buzz. Wandering through Logroño's night, this is wine country's most enchanting rhythm. Good night, Camino.

📚 Pilgrim's mini-guide: Logroño Wine Country & Calle Laurel

Logroño is the capital of the La Rioja region, the heart of Spain's wine industry, where Tempranillo grapes become world-class Rioja. The albergue connects to the church by underground tunnel, donation-based, registration only from 2pm.

Calle Laurel is known as the tapas road—each bar sells just one signature pincho, about €2-3 with a glass of red wine. Carrefour's wall of red wine runs €1-5.

🍄 Bar Soriano's mushroom pincho is the most famous on the street, worth any queue
🍷 A parasol mount is the 42°C survival tool—100% head-turn rate and still worth it
